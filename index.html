About the Game

All Roads Lead to Dagupan! Become the ultimate driver in Pangasinan with All Roads Lead to Dagupan! Manage your jeepney, pick up passengers across different towns, and earn enough to hit your daily quota. Keep an eye on your gas and vehicle health, turn on the radio for a chill ride, and unleash the 'Super Jeep Boost' to speed ahead. Para! Right here on the side!

🏙️ Landmarks

Drive past famous spots, from the historic Calasiao Church straight to the bustling streets of Dagupan.

📻 Radio Vibes

Jam to local radio stations and keep the good vibes rolling while cruising the streets.

How to Play

Master the wheel and become the ultimate jeepney driver of Dagupan!

🕹️

Driving Controls

  • WASD
    Paggalaw / Pagliko
  • R
    Start / Stop Engine
  • SPACE
    Busina (Horn)

Special Skills

  • L-CLICK
    Charge Super Jeep
  • RELEASE
    Activate Boost
  • E
    Toggle Headlights

Passenger & UI

  • F
    Magpababa ng Pasahero
  • ⬅️➡️
    Jeep Radio Music
  • ESC
    Reset / Main Menu
